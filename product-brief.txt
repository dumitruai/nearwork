Frontline Jobs Mobile App
Interview and freelancer test brief
Build a small mobile prototype that helps frontline workers discover nearby jobs through a Tinder-style swipe
experience. Think hospitality, retail, delivery, cleaning, or warehouse roles.
This brief is intentionally incomplete. Make reasonable product and technical decisions, and explain your
assumptions. We want to see how you turn a loose idea into a usable experience.
Core experience
● Let a worker provide a lightweight profile and job preferences. Choose which details are useful.
● Use device geolocation to match jobs by proximity. Location must affect the jobs shown or their order.
Show job distance and let the worker adjust how far they are willing to travel.
● Present jobs as swipeable cards with enough information to make a quick decision, such as role, employer,
pay, and working hours. Swipe left to pass and right to express interest.
● Simulate employer interest so some liked jobs become mutual matches. Let the worker revisit liked jobs
and matches. You decide the matching rules and how the result is presented.
Decide how the experience handles unavailable location, denied permission, and no suitable jobs. Emulator or
injected coordinates are acceptable for development, but include the device location integration.
Scope and data
Use realistic mocked jobs and employer responses. Include jobs at different coordinates so the effect of
changing location or travel distance can be demonstrated. The worker profile may also be mocked.
Choose your mobile stack and target one platform. Local data is sufficient; a backend, real authentication,
employer app, chat, maps, and notifications are not required.
Submission
Provide source code and a short README with setup instructions, assumptions, key tradeoffs, known
limitations, and time spent. Explain how to verify the swipe flow, simulated matches, and location-based
results.
Suggested timebox: 6 to 8 hours. If unfinished, submit a working core flow and describe what you would do
next.
What we will assess
A usable mobile experience, geolocation that meaningfully affects matching, clear and maintainable code, and
sensible judgment about scope and ambiguous requirements. Extra features are optional.
